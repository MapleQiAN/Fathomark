# Code of Conduct

We want Fathomark to be a respectful, evidence-oriented project. Participants
must be welcoming, assume good faith, give and receive specific technical
feedback, and keep discussions focused on the work rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation,
sexualized conduct, doxxing, threats, deliberate disruption, and publishing
private information without permission. Project maintainers may edit, remove,
or decline contributions that violate this standard and may ask a participant
to leave a discussion or repository.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
contact channel. Do not include secrets or personal data that is not needed to
handle the report.
